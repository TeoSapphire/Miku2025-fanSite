Snow Miku 2025 Event



Info

Every year in Hokkaidō, in collaboration with JR Tower (Sapporo), the Snow Miku event takes place. This year it's time for Snow Miku 2025!




Event

The Snow Miku 2025 event will be held at JR Tower and Odori Park in Sapporo. The program includes music performances, snow-themed shows, and the chance to purchase exclusive limited-edition goods. There will also be exhibitions, themed areas, and special booths dedicated to Snow Miku. For more information, visit the official website: snowmiku.com

  • Dates: February 8, 2025 / February 10, 2025
  • Time: 11:00 AM / 8:00 PM
